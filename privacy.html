Last Updated: June 11, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to our Inventory Management System. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal and business information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you use our platform.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

Account Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Company or business name
  • Phone number (if provided)

Business Data

  • Inventory records
  • Product information
  • Supplier information
  • Sales and purchase records
  • Reports generated within the system

Technical Information

  • Device information
  • Browser type
  • IP address
  • Usage statistics and activity logs

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain our services
  • Manage user accounts
  • Improve platform performance and functionality
  • Provide customer support
  • Ensure platform security
  • Communicate important service updates

Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no method of data transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may only be shared:

  • When required by law
  • To protect our legal rights
  • With trusted service providers necessary to operate the platform

Data Ownership

All inventory, supplier, sales, and business records entered into the platform remain the property of the customer.

We do not claim ownership of customer data.

Third-Party Services

Our platform may use third-party services to support hosting, database management, analytics, and security.

These providers may process data necessary to deliver our services.

Data Retention

We retain customer information only as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes become effective immediately upon publication on this page.
